03 / OPERATIONAL DEFINITIONS

Coding framework

The stages locate an intervention; the modes describe the user action. Codes are multi-label and modes are not an ordinal scale.

Four visual analytics stages

Five output-directed interaction modes

Read the consequence of the action.

Post-editing (P) changes a model-produced artifact locally or finally. Interactive editing (I) additionally requires the model to continue or regenerate from that direct edit. A follow-up prompt alone is guiding (G). User-led presentation authoring (U) applies only to PR.

The four-stage framework follows Luo et al.; the interaction modes adapt Cheng et al. (NAACL 2022) to visual analytics.

04 / DATA & METHODS

Data & methods

Snapshot v1.0.0

Download the records behind this explorer. The action-level crosswalk is the source for every stage–mode count shown here.

Scope & screening

55 candidate papers within January 2022–September 2026 were screened across TVCG, EuroVis, CHI, UIST, IUI, and IEEE PacificVis. The final corpus includes 46 systems reporting an active user action directed at an LLM-related artifact; 9 inspection-oriented candidates were excluded.

The 61-entry bibliography also contains excluded candidates and background literature. Its full venue totals are not the 46-paper corpus totals.

Evidence & reproducibility

Evidence notes reproduce the summaries supplied in the action-level crosswalk. They are coding summaries, not verbatim quotations. Full-text section/page locators are not supplied in the source CSV.

Statistics are computed from the consensus crosswalk. Independent coder labels are not included; this explorer does not recalculate agreement coefficients.

Formal citation metadata is read from the verified bibliography. Data Formulator uses the final 2024 TVCG issue year.

Screening audit: 9 excluded candidates

These records are excluded from the 46-paper corpus and from all intervention counts. The shared screening reason is the absence of a documented active output-directed action matching the coding scheme; paper-specific exclusion passages are not provided in the supplied CSV.
